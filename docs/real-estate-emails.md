## 1. To your broker

**Subject:** Quick question about referrals and online marketing

Hi [Broker's name],

I hope you're doing well. I'd like to focus my real estate work on referrals: acting as a trusted advisor for people I know, then connecting them with agents I trust. I'm not planning to take on buyers or sellers directly right now.

Before I do anything, I want to make sure I'm following your policies. Could you tell me:

1. Am I allowed to market myself online, such as on my website and social media, as a referral contact? If so, what has to be included (brokerage name, logo, license number)?
2. How do you handle referral fees, both paid to other brokerages and received from them? What is my split on a referral fee?
3. Is there a referral agreement form you'd like me to use?

Happy to talk by phone if that's easier. Thank you!

Rae
[Phone] · [License number]

## 2. To an agent you'd like to partner with

**Subject:** Would you be open to a referral partnership?

Hi [Agent's name],

I've always respected how you take care of your clients, and I'd love to send people your way.

I'm a licensed agent with [Brokerage], and I'm focusing on referrals. People come to me with real estate questions, and I want to connect them with someone I trust to give them great service.

Would you be open to a referral partnership? What I have in mind:
- I introduce clients who are ready to buy or sell in your area.
- A standard referral fee paid broker to broker, [25%] at closing.
- You keep me updated, so I can stay in my clients' corner.

Could we grab 15 minutes this week or next to talk it through?

Thank you,
Rae
[Phone]

---

**Tip:** send #1 first. Wait for your broker's answer before sending #2, so the referral terms you offer match their policy.
