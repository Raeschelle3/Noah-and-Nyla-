## Channel
- **Name:** What Life Carries
- **Handle idea:** @WhatLifeCarries (check it's available when creating)
- **Banner line:** Everyday conversations. Clearer understanding.
- **Look:** hunter green, warm cream, muted gold; cafe setting; speech-bubble icon.

**Description**
Sometimes the words sound clear, but the message still gets missed.

What Life Carries brings everyday adult moments to life through illustrated stories about relationships, boundaries, mixed signals, and starting again. Each episode shows a familiar moment, a useful question, and a clearer way to respond.

New short episodes every week. Subscribe for the next conversation.

These are fictional, AI-assisted illustrated stories made for reflection and discussion. They are not therapy or accounts of real people.

## Episodes
Use YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting honestly when uploading.

**01: They Text Every Day but Never Make Plans**
Description: Daily contact doesn't tell you whether your goals match. Ask clearly, then notice the follow-through. Subscribe for the next conversation. Fictional, AI-assisted illustrated story. #Relationships #Communication
Voiceover (about 30 sec): "They text every day. Good morning, good night, little check-ins. So why hasn't there been a single real plan? Daily contact feels close, but it doesn't tell you whether you want the same things. So ask clearly. Then watch what they do next. Follow-through says more than any text."

**02: You Want Commitment. They Want Casual.**
Description: You can like someone and still want different things. A clear conversation gives both people a chance to choose honestly. Fictional, AI-assisted illustrated story. #Dating #Communication
Voiceover: "You want a relationship. They want something casual. Neither of you is wrong, but you're not on the same road. Affection doesn't mean you want the same future. Believe what people tell you they want. Then choose what fits your life."

**03: The Words Sound Right. The Plans Keep Changing.**
Description: One change isn't a pattern. Repeated uncertainty is worth discussing. Fictional, AI-assisted illustrated story. #Relationships #Boundaries
Voiceover: "The words sound right. But the plans keep changing. One change isn't a pattern; life happens. But when it keeps happening, it's worth a conversation. Say what you've noticed. Then watch what changes after you talk. Patterns need context, and you deserve clarity."

## Production line (each episode)
1. **Claude:** story → 4 scenes in Canva (same character sheet) → voiceover script → captions.
2. **Rae (10 min):** drop each scene into **Runway** (image to video, 5 seconds, gentle motion: blink, phone glow, hair moving). Kaiber works too.
3. **Rae (5 min):** paste the script into ElevenLabs, export the voice.
4. **Claude:** cuts scenes + voice + music + captions + "WHAT LIFE CARRIES" end card into the 30-second Short.
5. **Rae:** upload, or Claude in Chrome uploads while she watches.
