# Noah & Nyla: Market Scan (Oct 30, 2026)

Key findings (full sources are in the session's research notes; YouTube help-page wording should be rechecked):

- **Ms Rachel's formula wins with parents:** slow speech, characters ask questions and pause 2–3 seconds, the target word repeated 3–5 times. Parents and speech therapists praise it for helping kids start talking.
- **Long compilations earn the money:** videos over 30 minutes were about 39% of uploads but 82% of watch time and 70% of revenue (Little Dot Studios analysis, quoted secondhand). Shorts get the channel found; 15–30 minute compilations build watch hours.
- **The AI backlash is real:** Bloomberg ("AI slop for babies," Dec 2025); about 200 groups asked YouTube to ban AI content from YouTube Kids (Apr 2026). YouTube keeps AI on YouTube Kids to a small set of high-quality channels.
- **Rules:** made-for-kids turns off comments, end screens, personalized ads and merch. The "inauthentic content" policy (July 2025) hits template spam, not AI itself. YPP needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
- **Money beyond ads:** printables ($1–$10, busy books $30–$40), songs on Spotify (via a distributor like DistroKid), later licensing. Sell to parents through Instagram, link-in-bio and an email list, since kids videos can't link out.

## Plan
1. **Named series:** "Count with Noah & Nyla," "Colors with Nyla," "Feelings Time." Same structure each time, new lesson.
2. **Ms Rachel-style scripts:** ask, pause, repeat. ElevenLabs at 0.95x (Noah = Rachel, Nyla = Pip).
3. **3–5 Shorts a week (30–60 sec);** after about 10 episodes, a weekly 15–30 minute compilation.
4. **Parent-friendly titles:** "Learn Colors for Toddlers | Noah & Nyla." Bright thumbnails with both faces and one object.
5. **No template spam:** a new script, scene and teaching moment every time.
6. **Made for kids on every video.**
7. **Parent funnel:** Instagram offers a free printable per episode, then a $5–$9 activity pack on Stan.
8. **Say it openly:** "Made with AI tools, guided by a real parent's and grandparent's lesson plan."
