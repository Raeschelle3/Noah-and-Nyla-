# LinkedIn → PMP Waitlist

**Goal:** 100 people on the January waitlist by Dec 15 (enough to fill 15 seats at a realistic conversion rate).
**Path:** LinkedIn post → link in post or comments → free Stan waitlist page → name + email → welcome email → enrollment opens when PMI approval or the format is confirmed.

## What Rae does (about 30 minutes, once)
1. **Create the waitlist on Stan.** Add a new **free** product that collects name and email (Stan's lead-magnet/"collect emails" type). Paste the copy below.
2. **Copy its link** and send it to Claude. It goes into every PMP post, the website "PMP" button, and your LinkedIn Featured section.
3. **LinkedIn profile:** update the headline and add the link to **Featured** (copy below).
4. **Post Mon/Wed** from the calendar in `docs/linkedin-campaign.md`. Reply to every comment within a day; that's what makes LinkedIn show the post to more people.

**Thank-you message:** You're on the list! Watch your inbox. I'll send the schedule and founding price before seats open to anyone else. — Rae

## LinkedIn profile
**Headline:** PMP, PMI-ACP | Helping project managers pass the PMP and lead with confidence | MBA
**Featured:** "January PMP Group: waitlist" → Stan waitlist link

## Each PMP post
- **First line is the hook** (it's all people see before "…see more").
- **End with one action:** "Join the January waitlist (link in the first comment)." Putting the link in the first comment keeps LinkedIn from showing the post to fewer people.
- **No claims** about PMI approval, "35 hours," or pass guarantees until approved.
