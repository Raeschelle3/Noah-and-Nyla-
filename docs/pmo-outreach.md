## LinkedIn post: AI + projects (Friday)

AI can write a status report in 10 seconds.

It can't tell you why your sponsor went quiet. It can't notice that the team stopped speaking up in standups. It can't walk into a room where a project is failing and get people to tell the truth.

Projects didn't stop because AI showed up. They got faster, and the human part of project management got more important:
→ Reading the room
→ Making the call when the data is incomplete
→ Keeping people moving when the plan changes

That's what the PMP really tests. I passed on my fourth try, because memorizing wasn't enough. I had to understand how projects work with real people.

In January I'm planning a small PMP exam-prep group for people who already have their 35 training hours, or who've taken the exam and want another shot.
