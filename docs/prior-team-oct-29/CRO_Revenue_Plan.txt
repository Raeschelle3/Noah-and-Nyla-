FUNNEL ASSUMPTIONS, NOT OBSERVED CONVERSION RATES
To sell 20 seats, a planning case of 20 percent conversion from qualified prospective learner to paid enrollee requires 100 qualified prospects. At 10 percent, it requires 200; at 5 percent, 400. A qualified prospect is an interested adult who understands the price, schedule, eligibility requirements, and intended course format and has consented to follow up.
LinkedIn impressions and followers are not qualified prospects. The first two weeks measure real interest and revise the funnel rather than assuming these percentages will occur.
Use three weekly LinkedIn posts: one practical project scenario, one learner question or preparation lesson, and one transparent invitation to express January interest. Offer a clear reply path. Respond to actual interest with eligibility and schedule questions; record consent and next action. No fabricated scarcity, pass guarantees, testimonials, or claims of approval.
