DEFINITIONS
Lead: One identifiable person who voluntarily expresses interest through a form, inquiry, or direct response. Count each person once per offer. Views and likes are not leads.
Qualified course lead: A lead whose desired course, January or February timing, likely eligibility needs, and willingness to consider the published price have been established. A conversation does not constitute PMI eligibility approval.
Enrollment: A person with an accepted registration for a specific cohort. Separate unpaid reservations from paid enrollment.
Paid enrollment: Registration matched to a settled, non-test payment. Record payment plan balances separately. Do not count an entire payment plan as cash collected.
Gross cash collected: Actual customer payments received in the period before refunds and processing fees. Exclude test payments and failed or pending payments.
Gross earned revenue: Revenue for services or products delivered during the period, before refunds and expenses. Advance course payments are tracked separately from delivered training; confirm accounting treatment with the owner's accountant.
Refunds: Actual customer amounts returned in the period. Track requests and approved but unpaid refunds separately.
Net cash receipts: Gross cash collected less refunds and chargeback amounts withdrawn. Show payment processing fees separately to avoid confusing receipts with profit.
Contribution: Net earned revenue less directly attributable delivery, materials, payment, and acquisition costs. If costs are missing, report Not available, not an estimated profit.
Personal take home: Owner compensation or withdrawals after business obligations. It is not equivalent to gross revenue.

DECISION RULES
Few relevant inquiries: test audience, problem statement, and opening message before increasing spending.
Inquiries but few qualified leads: clarify eligibility, intended audience, timing, and offer scope.
Qualified leads but few paid enrollments: review objections, schedule, price, approval status, and checkout friction. Do not assume price is the sole cause.
Payments without delivery capacity: reduce available seats or adjust delivery before expanding promotion.
Views without product visits: test the relevance and clarity of the call to action and confirm the destination works.
Visits without purchases: review product fit, offer details, checkout, and source quality. Insufficient data is a valid conclusion.
Strong engagement without attributed sales: report engagement success only. It is not proven commercial success.
