POST 2 | WEDNESDAY NOVEMBER 4 | RISK
A risk register cannot do the work of a conversation.

If your project update says “vendor delay,” ask a better set of questions:
What could cause the delay?
Which milestone could it affect?
Who owns the response?
What signal tells us it is time to act?

Try this sentence:
“If the supplier misses the review date, testing may start late. Our response owner is ____. We will act when ____.”

That gives the team something it can use.

What risk keeps showing up on your projects without a clear owner?

#RiskManagement #ProjectManagement #PMP

POST 4 | MONDAY NOVEMBER 9 | STUDY ORGANIZATION
If every study session starts with “What should I work on today?” you are spending energy before you even begin.

Give the next session one clear purpose.
Choose a topic. Work through a scenario. Explain why you chose your answer. Write down what you missed and return to it.

A useful study plan tells you what happens next. It also leaves room to review what you did not understand the first time.

POST 5 | WEDNESDAY NOVEMBER 11 | REAL ESTATE PROCESS EDUCATION
A real estate decision involves more than finding a property you like.

There are documents, deadlines, questions, and people responsible for the next step. When any of those are unclear, it becomes harder to make an informed decision.

One useful habit: keep a simple decision log.
What do I need to know?
Who can verify it?
When do I need the answer?
What decision depends on that answer?

This is an organization tip, not a substitute for advice from your transaction professionals. I am sharing education here, not announcing a real estate class.

#RealEstate #DecisionMaking #Organization

POST 6 | FRIDAY NOVEMBER 13 | PMP AND PRACTICAL THINKING
Knowing a definition and making a decision are different skills.

When you practice a project management scenario, slow down long enough to ask:
What is actually happening?
What information is missing?
Who needs to be involved?
What should happen next, and why?

Then explain your reasoning in plain language. If you cannot explain it, that is a useful place to study further.
